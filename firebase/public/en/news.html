Updates

8 November 2026

IndexNow, new footer and translated pages

The site now notifies search engines as soon as a page changes. Every page has a shared footer, and the home page introduction is translated into English and Russian.

7 November 2026

Live server list

A new page shows every server's status in real time: available, starting or in a match, with the map and score. See the server list.

7 November 2026

Players now vote for the 5v5 maps

The competitive pool is now chosen by the community: every month you vote for 1 to 7 maps out of 12, and the 7 most voted make up next month's rotation. First rotation on December 1. Unranked mode is removed to keep servers for ranked play.

7 November 2026

1v1 Arena: its own lounge and ranks

1v1 Aim now has its own lounge, ladder and ranks, from Iron to 128 Legend. You start at 1000 ELO, in Bronze II.
